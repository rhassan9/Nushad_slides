* Teams, People and Performance -
* Module Aims

The aim of this module is to provide students with an understanding of the principles and practices that underpin effective teamwork and team performance. Students will explore the roles and dynamics within teams, recognising how individual contributions and leadership impact collective outcomes. The module seeks to develop students’ abilities to identify and apply key team theories and practices in real-world scenarios. Through engaging activities and assessments, students will gain insights into team development, communication, and performance management, equipping them with the foundational skills needed to effectively collaborate and contribute within diverse organisational settings.

---

### Strengths:

* Extrovert, enthusiastic, communicative.
* Explores opportunities; develops contacts

### Allowable weaknesses:

* Over optimistic.
* Loses interest once initial enthusiasm has passed

### Resource Investigator:

---

### Strengths:

* Mature, confident, a good chair-person.
* Clarifies goals, promotes decision making, delegates well

### Allowable weaknesses:

* Can be seen as manipulative. Delegates personal work

### Co-ordinator:

---

### Strengths:

* Challenging, dynamic, thrives on pressure.
* Has the drive and courage to overcome obstacles

### Allowable weaknesses:

* Can provoke others.
* Tends to hurt people’s
* feelings

### Shaper:

---

### Strengths:

* Sober, strategic and discerning.
* Sees all options.
* Judges accurately

### Allowable weaknesses:

* Lacks drive and ability
* to inspire others.
* Overly critical

### Monitor Evaluator:

---

### Strengths:

* Co-operative, mild, perceptive and diplomatic.
* Listens, builds, averts friction, calms the waters

### Allowable weaknesses:

* Indecisive in crunch
* situations.
* Can be easily influenced

### Team worker:

---

### Strengths:

* Disciplined, reliable, conservative and efficient.
* Turns ideas into practical actions

### Allowable weaknesses:

* Somewhat inflexible.
* Slow to respond to new
* possibilities

### Implementer:

---

### Strengths:

* Painstakingly conscientious, anxious.
* Searches out errors and omissions.
* Delivers on time

### Allowable weaknesses:

* Inclined to worry unduly.
* Reluctant to delegate.
* Can be a nit-picker

### Completer/finisher:

---

### Strengths:

* Single-minded, self-starting, dedicated.
* Provides knowledge and skills in short supply

### Allowable weaknesses:

* Contributes on only a narrow front
* Dwells on technicalities
* Overlooks the ‘big picture’.
